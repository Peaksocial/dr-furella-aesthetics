Overview

If you have been out of the aesthetics industry for some time and feel your skills need refreshing, but do not require starting from scratch, our customised Refresher Day is designed for that.

Our Refresher Days are completely bespoke and tailored to your skill level, experience, and specific training needs. Simply tell us what you would like to focus on, and we will design a personalised training plan that fits comfortably within a full day without feeling rushed. We will organise models for you (or you are welcome to bring your own) and ensure you leave updated and ready to practise again.

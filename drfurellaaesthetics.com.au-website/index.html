Doctor-Led Care

How we practise

Experience the Dr Furella difference.

Dr Furella Aesthetics is a doctor-run clinic. Cosmetic procedures are medical procedures and we treat them that way. Every patient is assessed individually by a registered practitioner, and a procedure only goes ahead where it is clinically appropriate for that person.

We practise conservatively. Where a procedure is indicated, we use the least invasive option that meets the clinical need, and we will tell you when we think no treatment is the right answer. Ageing and normal variation in the face and body are not conditions that require treatment.

Questions

Good to know.

Anything else on your mind? Our team is happy to talk it through before you book.

Where are your clinics located?

We have two doctor-run clinics: 146 Pennant Street, Parramatta NSW 2151 (Sydney) and 15 Bourke Street, Maitland NSW 2320 in the Hunter Valley. You can book a consultation at either location.

Is Dr Furella Aesthetics a doctor-run clinic?

Yes. We are a doctor-run aesthetics, body contour and skin clinic. From your first consultation to your final follow-up you are guided by registered medical practitioners and nurses, and every treatment is planned and delivered by our medical team.

What results can I expect?

Outcomes vary between individuals and cannot be guaranteed. What is realistically achievable for you, along with the limitations and the risks, is assessed and discussed at your consultation before anything is decided.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes. Every treatment plan is customised to your goals and skin, so we begin with a thorough consultation to assess your concerns, discuss your options, and design a personalised plan with clear, transparent guidance.

Do you offer training for practitioners?

We do. We run doctor-led, accredited training and CPD workshops for doctors, nurses, nurse practitioners and dentists, from foundation cosmetic injectables through to advanced full-face and body contouring, PDO threads, IV therapy, sclerotherapy, Platelet Derived Therapy and laser.

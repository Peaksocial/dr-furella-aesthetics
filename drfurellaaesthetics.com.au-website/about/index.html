About Us
A doctor-ledaesthetics clinic.

Considered, doctor-led aesthetic care.

At Dr Furella Aesthetics, our foundation is our team of dedicated medical professionals. We are a doctor-run clinic, which means from your first consultation to your final follow-up, you are guided by registered medical practitioners.

Our Team

Your doctor-led team.

Our doctors and nurses are registered health practitioners working within their scope of practice. Every appointment starts with a consultation and an examination, so that any recommendation is based on your medical history and your individual circumstances rather than on a treatment menu.

Where a procedure is clinically appropriate, you are given the information you need to make an informed decision about it: what is involved, the risks, the recovery, the cost, and the alternatives.

Our Philosophy & Values

How we practise

Dr Furella Aesthetics is a doctor-run clinic. Cosmetic procedures are medical procedures and we treat them that way. Every patient is assessed individually by a registered practitioner, and a procedure only goes ahead where it is clinically appropriate for that person.

We practise conservatively. Where a procedure is indicated, we use the least invasive option that meets the clinical need, and we will tell you when we think no treatment is the right answer. Ageing and normal variation in the face and body are not conditions that require treatment.

All cosmetic procedures carry risks. Before you decide anything, your practitioner will go through what the procedure can and cannot do, the risks and possible complications, the expected recovery, the cost, and the alternatives, including having no treatment at all. You will be given written information and time to consider it.

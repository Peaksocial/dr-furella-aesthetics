Subcision

Considered, doctor-led aesthetic care.

A minor procedure that releases tethered, depressed scars so the skin can lift back towards its natural position.

Book NowView Pricing

Subcision releases tethered, depressed scar attachments so the skin can lift back towards its natural position. Most scarring is treated in one session, while moderate to severe cases may need two or three. Any procedure is carried out only where the doctor considers it appropriate for you.

Important information

A clinical consultation is required before treatment. Your practitioner will assess your concerns, relevant medical history, treatment suitability and available options. Potential risks, limitations, expected recovery and realistic treatment outcomes are also discussed as part of the informed consent process.

Treatment is not suitable for everyone. Suitability and outcomes vary between individuals.
